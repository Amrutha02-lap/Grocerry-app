🎧 Help & Support

How do I return an item?

If you received a damaged or incorrect item, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery, and we will issue a refund or replace the item immediately.

What are the delivery charges?

Delivery is completely FREE for orders above ₹199. For smaller orders, a flat ₹20 fee applies.

How do I cancel my order?

You can call us to cancel your order as long as it has not been marked "Out for Delivery".
